PRIVACY POLICY
Train Seat

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Train Seat (“the app”) helps users view Bangladesh Railway train schedules and seat availability and access the Bangladesh Railway e-ticketing service. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

INFORMATION HANDLED

Bangladesh Railway e-ticketing service
The app displays the Bangladesh Railway e-ticketing website (eticket.railway.gov.bd) in an embedded web browser (WebView). When you use that service, Bangladesh Railway may collect and process information you provide, such as your account credentials, contact and passenger details, journey information, and booking or payment information. The Railway service may also use cookies and similar technologies to authenticate you and operate its website. This information is handled by Bangladesh Railway under its own privacy practices and terms.

Authentication and session information
To keep you signed in and support train and seat-availability requests, the app may read authentication and device-session values from the Railway website and store those values in the device’s secure storage. The app uses them to make requests to the Railway service on your behalf. WebView cookies and website storage may also be used for the Railway service to work. Do not share your device with people you do not trust, and sign out of the Railway service when appropriate.

Train and seat-availability requests
When you check train schedules or seat availability, the app may send the requested journey information and your session authentication values to Bangladesh Railway’s servers. The app receives the resulting information to display it to you. Train schedule information may also be bundled with the app.

Advertising
The app displays non-personalized ads through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may process information such as device or ad identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostic information to provide and measure ads and help prevent fraud. For more information, see Google’s privacy policy and information about how Google uses data from sites or apps that use its services.

HOW INFORMATION IS USED

Information is used to:
- Provide the app’s train schedule and seat-availability features.
- Display the Bangladesh Railway e-ticketing service and maintain its authenticated session.
- Show non-personalized advertisements and support the app.
- Maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the app.

SHARING

The app does not sell your personal information. Information entered into the Railway website, and session information used for Railway requests, is sent to Bangladesh Railway to provide its services. Advertising-related information may be processed by Google AdMob as described above. These third parties handle information under their own privacy policies.

DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

Authentication and device-session values stored by the app remain on your device to support your session until they are overwritten or removed. You can clear the app’s stored data or uninstall the app to remove locally stored app data; this may not delete information held by Bangladesh Railway or Google.

To delete your e-ticket account or associated data, please contact the Bangladesh Railway e-ticketing support channel or visit their official website portal.

SECURITY

The app stores session values using the device’s secure storage facilities where available and uses the Railway service over its website connection. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and the app cannot control the security practices of Bangladesh Railway or other third parties.

CHILDREN’S PRIVACY

The app is not intended to collect personal information directly from children. The Railway e-ticketing service and advertising providers may apply their own age-related requirements and privacy practices.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Use of the Bangladesh Railway e-ticketing service is subject to Bangladesh Railway’s terms and privacy practices. Ads are provided by Google AdMob and are subject to Google’s terms and privacy policy. Please review those providers’ current notices for details about their collection and use of information.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

This policy may be updated when the app or its practices change. The latest version should be made available through the app’s store listing or the website where this policy is published.

CONTACT

For questions about this app’s privacy practices, use the developer contact information shown on the app’s Google Play listing.
